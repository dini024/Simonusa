D1.1 Gagasan: kerapatan elektron sebagai variabel dasar

D1.1 The idea: electron density as the basic variable

Fungsi gelombang $\Psi$ punya $3N$ dimensi (Bab 2–4), sedangkan kerapatan $\rho(\mathbf r)=N\int|\Psi|^2\,d\mathbf r_2\cdots d\mathbf r_N$ selalu 3 dimensi, dengan $\int\rho\,d\mathbf r=N$.

The wave function $\Psi$ has $3N$ dimensions (Chapters 2–4), whereas the density $\rho(\mathbf r)=N\int|\Psi|^2\,d\mathbf r_2\cdots d\mathbf r_N$ is always 3-dimensional, with $\int\rho\,d\mathbf r=N$.

  • Teorema HK-1 (1964): $\rho_0(\mathbf r)$ keadaan dasar menentukan secara unik potensial eksternal $v_{ext}$ (sampai konstanta), sehingga seluruh sifat sistem: $E_0=E[\rho_0]$.
  • Theorem HK-1 (1964): the ground-state $\rho_0(\mathbf r)$ uniquely determines the external potential $v_{ext}$ (up to a constant), and hence all properties of the system: $E_0=E[\rho_0]$.
  • Teorema HK-2: untuk $\rho$ uji yang valid, $E[\rho]\ge E_0$ (prinsip variasional): $\rho_0$ dicari dengan meminimalkan $E[\rho]$.
  • Theorem HK-2: for any valid trial $\rho$, $E[\rho]\ge E_0$ (variational principle): $\rho_0$ is found by minimising $E[\rho]$.

Teorema ini hanya menjamin keberadaan fungsional; bentuk eksaknya tidak diketahui.

These theorems only guarantee the existence of the functional; its exact form is unknown.

D1.2 Persamaan Kohn–Sham (1965)

D1.2 The Kohn–Sham equations (1965)

$$E[\rho]=\underbrace{T_s[\{\varphi_i\}]}_{\color{#d33}{\text{kinetik sistem tak berinteraksi}}}+\underbrace{\int v_{ext}\rho\,d\mathbf r}_{\color{#1a6fd4}{\text{tarikan inti}}}+\underbrace{J[\rho]}_{\color{#1a9a4a}{\text{Hartree (Coulomb klasik)}}}+\underbrace{E_{xc}[\rho]}_{\text{pertukaran + korelasi + koreksi kinetik}}$$
$$E[\rho]=\underbrace{T_s[\{\varphi_i\}]}_{\color{#d33}{\text{kinetic energy of the non-interacting system}}}+\underbrace{\int v_{ext}\rho\,d\mathbf r}_{\color{#1a6fd4}{\text{nuclear attraction}}}+\underbrace{J[\rho]}_{\color{#1a9a4a}{\text{Hartree (classical Coulomb)}}}+\underbrace{E_{xc}[\rho]}_{\text{exchange + correlation + kinetic correction}}$$
$$\Big[-\tfrac12\nabla^2+v_{ext}(\mathbf r)+\underbrace{\int\tfrac{\rho(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}d\mathbf r'}_{v_H[\rho]}+\underbrace{\tfrac{\delta E_{xc}}{\delta\rho}}_{v_{xc}[\rho]}\Big]\varphi_i=\varepsilon_i\varphi_i,\qquad \rho=\sum_i^{occ}|\varphi_i|^2$$

Sama seperti HF, potensial efektif bergantung pada $\rho$ yang berasal dari orbital, sehingga diselesaikan dengan SCF. Bedanya: HF memakai pertukaran eksak dan tanpa korelasi; KS memakai $E_{xc}$ hampiran yang memuat keduanya (fungsional hibrida mencampur sebagian pertukaran HF).

As in HF, the effective potential depends on $\rho$, which comes from the orbitals, so it is solved by SCF. The difference: HF uses exact exchange and no correlation; KS uses an approximate $E_{xc}$ that contains both (hybrid functionals mix in some HF exchange).

D1.3 SCF pada kerapatan: pembentukan ikatan H₂

D1.3 SCF on the density: the formation of the H₂ bond

Animasi memakai SCF H₂ nyata dari Bab 4 (R=1,4 bohr), dilihat sebagai kerapatan sepanjang sumbu ikatan. Garis abu-abu = penjumlahan dua atom H yang belum berinteraksi; garis biru = kerapatan molekul pada iterasi berjalan; kurva bawah = selisih (kerapatan menumpuk di antara inti = ikatan kovalen). Ini struktur yang sama dengan siklus KS: ρ → potensial → orbital → ρ baru.

The animation uses the real H₂ SCF from Chapter 4 (R=1.4 bohr), viewed as the density along the bond axis. Grey line = the sum of two non-interacting H atoms; blue line = the molecular density at the current iteration; lower curve = the difference (density accumulates between the nuclei = covalent bond). This is the same structure as the KS cycle: ρ → potential → orbitals → new ρ.

D1.4 Konsekuensi praktis

D1.4 Practical consequences

  • Orbital KS bukan orbital "fisik", tetapi bentuknya sangat mirip orbital HF dan umum dipakai untuk analisis HOMO/LUMO.
  • KS orbitals are not "physical" orbitals, but their shapes are very similar to HF orbitals and they are widely used for HOMO/LUMO analysis.
  • Biaya DFT murni/GGA ~N³ (lebih rendah dari HF), sehingga cocok untuk ratusan atom.
  • The cost of pure/GGA DFT scales ~N³ (lower than HF), so hundreds of atoms are feasible.
  • Seluruh kualitas hasil ditentukan oleh $E_{xc}$ → bab D2.
  • The whole quality of the result is set by $E_{xc}$ → chapter D2.
  • Kerapatan keadaan dasar saja tidak memberi eksitasi; untuk itu dipakai TD-DFT (bab D6).
  • The ground-state density alone gives no excitations; for that TD-DFT is used (chapter D6).
