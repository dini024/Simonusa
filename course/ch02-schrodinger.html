2.1 Sejarah: mengapa fisika klasik tidak cukup

2.1 History: why classical physics was not enough

1900Planck: energi radiasi benda hitam terkuantisasi, E = hν.Planck: blackbody radiation energy is quantised, E = hν.
1905Einstein: efek fotolistrik, cahaya datang dalam paket (foton).Einstein: the photoelectric effect; light arrives in packets (photons).
1913Bohr: orbit elektron hidrogen berenergi diskret; menjelaskan garis spektrum, tetapi ad hoc.Bohr: the hydrogen electron has discrete orbit energies; this explains the line spectrum, but ad hoc.
1924de Broglie: materi punya panjang gelombang, λ = h/p.de Broglie: matter has a wavelength, λ = h/p.
1925Heisenberg: mekanika matriks.Heisenberg: matrix mechanics.
1926Schrödinger: mekanika gelombang, kuantisasi sebagai masalah nilai eigen; terbukti setara dengan mekanika matriks. Born menafsirkan |Ψ|² sebagai rapat peluang.Schrödinger: wave mechanics, quantisation as an eigenvalue problem; proven equivalent to matrix mechanics. Born interprets |Ψ|² as a probability density.
1933Nobel Fisika untuk Schrödinger dan Dirac.Nobel Prize in Physics for Schrödinger and Dirac.

Mengapa jadi dasar? Ia memberi satu persamaan umum yang, jika diselesaikan, menghasilkan energi diskret dan fungsi gelombang untuk atom dan molekul. Semua metode di course ini (HF, DFT, CC) adalah cara menyelesaikannya secara hampiran. Dirac (1929): hukum fisika untuk kimia sudah diketahui, kesulitannya adalah persamaan terlalu rumit untuk diselesaikan eksak.

Why is it the foundation? It provides one general equation which, when solved, yields the discrete energies and wave functions of atoms and molecules. All methods in this course (HF, DFT, CC) are approximate ways of solving it. Dirac (1929): the physical laws for chemistry are known; the difficulty is that the equations are too complicated to solve exactly.

2.2 Persamaan dan artinya

2.2 The equation and its meaning

$$\left[\underbrace{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2}_{\color{#d33}{\text{energi kinetik: kelengkungan }\psi}}+\underbrace{V(\mathbf r)}_{\color{#1a6fd4}{\text{energi potensial}}}\right]\psi=\underbrace{E}_{\color{#1a9a4a}{\text{energi (nilai eigen)}}}\psi$$
$$\left[\underbrace{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2}_{\color{#d33}{\text{kinetic energy: curvature of }\psi}}+\underbrace{V(\mathbf r)}_{\color{#1a6fd4}{\text{potential energy}}}\right]\psi=\underbrace{E}_{\color{#1a9a4a}{\text{energy (eigenvalue)}}}\psi$$
  • Kinetik: makin melengkung (banyak osilasi) fungsi gelombang, makin tinggi energinya (lihat bagian 2.4).
  • Kinetic: the more curved (oscillating) the wave function, the higher its energy (see Section 2.4).
  • Potensial: lingkungan partikel (inti, dinding kotak).
  • Potential: the particle's surroundings (nucleus, box walls).
  • Nilai eigen E: hanya nilai tertentu yang menghasilkan ψ yang bisa dinormalkan, itulah asal kuantisasi.
  • Eigenvalue E: only certain values give a normalisable ψ; this is the origin of quantisation.
  • Bergantung waktu: $i\hbar\,\partial_t\Psi=\hat H\Psi$; keadaan eigen hanya berputar fase: $\Psi_n(t)=\psi_n e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$.
  • Time-dependent: $i\hbar\,\partial_t\Psi=\hat H\Psi$; an eigenstate only rotates in phase: $\Psi_n(t)=\psi_n e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$.
  • Tafsir Born: $P(x)=|\Psi(x,t)|^2$, dengan $\int|\Psi|^2dx=1$.
  • Born interpretation: $P(x)=|\Psi(x,t)|^2$, with $\int|\Psi|^2dx=1$.

2.3 Paket gelombang bergerak

2.3 The moving wave packet

Partikel bebas (V=0) digambarkan paket gelombang: bagian real dan imajiner berosilasi (fase), |Ψ|² adalah peluang. Perhatikan paket bergerak ke kanan dan melebar (dispersi), konsekuensi langsung persamaan Schrödinger (satuan atom: ħ=m=1, k₀=2).

A free particle (V=0) is described by a wave packet: the real and imaginary parts oscillate (phase), and |Ψ|² is the probability. Notice that the packet moves to the right and spreads (dispersion), a direct consequence of the Schrödinger equation (atomic units: ħ=m=1, k₀=2).

$$\Psi(x,t)=\frac{e^{ik_0x-ik_0^2t/2}}{\pi^{1/4}\sqrt{1+it}}\exp\!\left[-\frac{(x-k_0t)^2}{2(1+it)}\right]$$

2.4 Particle in a box: quantisation and superposition

Pilih dua keadaan dan komposisinya. Satu keadaan eigen: kerapatan diam. Campuran dua keadaan: kerapatan "berayun" dengan frekuensi $\omega=(E_2-E_1)/\hbar$. Gambar bawah memperlihatkan Ψ sebagai kurva bergerak dan peluang pengukuran.
