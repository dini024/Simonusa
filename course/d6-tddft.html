D6.1 Teori singkat

D6.1 Short theory

TD-DFT memberi energi eksitasi sebagai pole respons linear kerapatan (persamaan Casida):

TD-DFT gives excitation energies as the poles of the linear density response (the Casida equation):

$$\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf A&\mathbf B\\\mathbf B&\mathbf A\end{pmatrix}\!\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf X\\\mathbf Y\end{pmatrix}=\omega\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}\!\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf X\\\mathbf Y\end{pmatrix},\qquad A_{ia,jb}=\delta_{ij}\delta_{ab}(\varepsilon_a-\varepsilon_i)+K_{ia,jb}$$

Pendekatan Tamm–Dancoff (TDA) menetapkan $\mathbf B=0$. Intensitas ditentukan kekuatan osilator: $f_{0n}=\tfrac23\,\omega_{0n}\,|\langle0|\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}|n\rangle|^2$ (satuan atom), dan $\lambda\,[\text{nm}]=1239{,}84/E\,[\text{eV}]$.

The Tamm–Dancoff approximation (TDA) sets $\mathbf B=0$. The intensity is set by the oscillator strength: $f_{0n}=\tfrac23\,\omega_{0n}\,|\langle0|\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}|n\rangle|^2$ (atomic units), and $\lambda\,[\text{nm}]=1239.84/E\,[\text{eV}]$.

D6.3 Praktik dan jebakan

D6.3 Practice and pitfalls

  • Gunakan geometri keadaan dasar hasil optimasi tanpa frekuensi imajiner, lalu TD-DFT dengan pelarut (CPCM/SMD) bila eksperimen dalam larutan.
  • Use the ground-state geometry from an optimisation without imaginary frequencies, then TD-DFT with solvent (CPCM/SMD) if the experiment is in solution.
  • Jumlah state harus cukup menutup rentang spektrum (mis. 20–40), dan periksa state dengan f besar.
  • The number of states must cover the spectral range (e.g. 20–40); check the states with large f.
  • Fungsional hibrida murni meremehkan energi eksitasi transfer muatan (CT); gunakan range-separated (CAM-B3LYP, ωB97X-D) dan bandingkan.
  • Pure hybrid functionals underestimate charge-transfer (CT) excitation energies; use range-separated functionals (CAM-B3LYP, ωB97X-D) and compare.
  • Transisi diberi label orbital (mis. HOMO→LUMO, koefisien). Koefisien² ≈ kontribusi.
  • Transitions carry orbital labels (e.g. HOMO→LUMO, coefficient). Coefficient² ≈ contribution.
  • Dalam literatur DSSC komputasi sering dipakai $\mathrm{LHE}=1-10^{-f}$ untuk memperkirakan efisiensi penangkapan cahaya satu transisi.
  • In the computational DSSC literature, $\mathrm{LHE}=1-10^{-f}$ is often used to estimate the light-harvesting efficiency of a single transition.
  • Eksperimen memuat lebar vibronik; bandingkan pola dan λmax, bukan hanya angka tunggal.
  • Experiments contain vibronic broadening; compare the pattern and λmax, not just a single number.
