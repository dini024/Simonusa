D2.3 Dispersi: mengapa DFT perlu koreksi

D2.3 Dispersion: why DFT needs a correction

Gaya dispersi (London) berasal dari korelasi jangka panjang yang tidak tertangkap fungsional lokal. Koreksi D3 menambah suku empiris dengan peredam Becke–Johnson agar tidak divergen pada jarak dekat:

London dispersion forces come from long-range correlation that local functionals do not capture. The D3 correction adds an empirical term with Becke–Johnson damping so that it does not diverge at short range:
