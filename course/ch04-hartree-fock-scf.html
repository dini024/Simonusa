4.1 Hamiltonian molekul dan masalahnya

4.1 The molecular Hamiltonian and its problem

Dengan pendekatan Born–Oppenheimer (inti diam karena jauh lebih berat), Hamiltonian elektronik (satuan atom):

With the Born–Oppenheimer approximation (nuclei fixed because they are much heavier), the electronic Hamiltonian (atomic units):

$$\hat H_{el}=\underbrace{-\sum_i\tfrac12\nabla_i^2}_{\color{#d33}{\text{kinetik}}}\;\underbrace{-\sum_{i,A}\frac{Z_A}{r_{iA}}}_{\color{#1a6fd4}{\text{tarikan inti}}}\;+\underbrace{\sum_{i\lt j}\frac1{r_{ij}}}_{\color{#1a9a4a}{\text{tolakan elektron-elektron}}}$$
$$\hat H_{el}=\underbrace{-\sum_i\tfrac12\nabla_i^2}_{\color{#d33}{\text{kinetic}}}\;\underbrace{-\sum_{i,A}\frac{Z_A}{r_{iA}}}_{\color{#1a6fd4}{\text{nuclear attraction}}}\;+\underbrace{\sum_{i\lt j}\frac1{r_{ij}}}_{\color{#1a9a4a}{\text{electron–electron repulsion}}}$$

Suku hijau mengikat semua elektron sehingga persamaan tidak bisa dipisah per elektron. Hartree–Fock (HF) menggantinya dengan tolakan rata-rata: tiap elektron bergerak dalam medan rata-rata elektron lain.

The green term couples all electrons, so the equation cannot be separated per electron. Hartree–Fock (HF) replaces it with an average repulsion: each electron moves in the average field of the other electrons.

4.2 Fungsi gelombang: determinan Slater

4.2 The wave function: the Slater determinant

$$\Psi=\frac1{\sqrt{N!}}\begin{vmatrix}\chi_1(x_1)&\chi_2(x_1)&\cdots\\\chi_1(x_2)&\chi_2(x_2)&\cdots\\\vdots&\vdots&\ddots\end{vmatrix}$$

Menukar dua elektron = menukar dua baris = tanda determinan berbalik, sehingga Ψ antisimetrik (asas Pauli). Dua elektron dengan spin-orbital sama membuat dua kolom identik dan determinan = 0.

Swapping two electrons = swapping two rows = the sign of the determinant flips, so Ψ is antisymmetric (Pauli principle). Two electrons with the same spin-orbital make two columns identical and the determinant = 0.

4.3 Persamaan Hartree–Fock–Roothaan

4.3 The Hartree–Fock–Roothaan equations

$$\mathbf F\mathbf C=\mathbf S\mathbf C\boldsymbol\varepsilon,\qquad F_{\mu\nu}=H^{core}_{\mu\nu}+\sum_{\lambda\sigma}P_{\lambda\sigma}\Big[(\mu\nu|\sigma\lambda)-\tfrac12(\mu\lambda|\sigma\nu)\Big],\qquad P_{\lambda\sigma}=2\sum_{i}^{occ}C_{\lambda i}C_{\sigma i}$$ $$E=\tfrac12\sum_{\mu\nu}P_{\mu\nu}\big(H^{core}_{\mu\nu}+F_{\mu\nu}\big)+V_{nn}$$
  • Hcore: kinetik + tarikan inti. Suku (μν|σλ): Coulomb (tolakan awan elektron). Suku (μλ|σν): pertukaran (efek Pauli, tanpa analog klasik).
  • Hcore: kinetic + nuclear attraction. The term (μν|σλ): Coulomb (repulsion of the electron cloud). The term (μλ|σν): exchange (Pauli effect, no classical analogue).
  • Kunci: F bergantung pada P, P bergantung pada C, C berasal dari F. Persamaan "melingkar", jadi harus diselesaikan iteratif: SCF (self-consistent field).
  • Key point: F depends on P, P depends on C, and C comes from F. The equation is "circular", so it must be solved iteratively: SCF (self-consistent field).

4.5 Batas Hartree–Fock

4.5 The Hartree–Fock limit

  • HF memenuhi Pauli tetapi menganggap elektron hanya merasakan rata-rata yang lain. Hilangnya korelasi gerak elektron adalah energi korelasi (biasanya ≈ 1% energi total, tetapi sebanding dengan energi ikatan kimia).
  • HF satisfies the Pauli principle but assumes each electron only feels the average of the others. The missing correlation of electron motion is the correlation energy (usually ≈ 1% of the total energy, but comparable to chemical bond energies).
  • Akibatnya HF gagal pada disosiasi ikatan, kompleks logam, dan energi reaksi akurat. Bab 5 membahas perbaikannya (MP2, CI, Coupled Cluster), dan Course 2 membahas DFT yang memasukkan korelasi dengan biaya lebih rendah.
  • As a result HF fails for bond dissociation, metal complexes and accurate reaction energies. Chapter 5 covers the remedies (MP2, CI, Coupled Cluster), and Course 2 covers DFT, which includes correlation at lower cost.
  • Praktik: SCF sulit konvergen → turunkan mixing/damping, pakai level shift, DIIS, atau tebakan awal dari struktur/orbital sebelumnya.
  • In practice: if SCF is hard to converge → lower the mixing/damping, use a level shift, DIIS, or an initial guess from a previous structure/orbitals.
