8.1 Permukaan energi potensial (PES)

8.1 The potential energy surface (PES)

Dengan Born–Oppenheimer, energi elektronik adalah fungsi posisi inti: $E(\mathbf R)$. Titik stasioner ($\nabla E=0$): minimum (semua kelengkungan positif; reaktan, produk, intermediet) atau titik pelana orde-1 (satu kelengkungan negatif; keadaan transisi).

With Born–Oppenheimer, the electronic energy is a function of the nuclear positions: $E(\mathbf R)$. Stationary points ($\nabla E=0$): a minimum (all curvatures positive; reactants, products, intermediates) or a first-order saddle point (one negative curvature; a transition state).

8.2 Frekuensi harmonik dari kelengkungan

8.2 Harmonic frequencies from curvature

$$\tilde\nu=\frac1{2\pi c}\sqrt{\frac{k}{\mu}}\approx130{,}28\sqrt{\frac{k\,[\text{N/m}]}{\mu\,[\text{amu}]}}\ \text{cm}^{-1},\qquad E_{ZPE}=\tfrac12h c\tilde\nu$$

k = turunan kedua energi (Hessian), μ = massa tereduksi. Geser k dan μ: makin kaku ikatan atau makin ringan atom, makin tinggi frekuensi. Contoh CO: k≈1850 N/m, μ≈6,86 amu → ≈2140 cm⁻¹. Gerak di bawah diperlambat agar terlihat.

k = the second derivative of the energy (Hessian), μ = reduced mass. Move k and μ: the stiffer the bond or the lighter the atoms, the higher the frequency. Example CO: k≈1850 N/m, μ≈6.86 amu → ≈2140 cm⁻¹. The motion below is slowed down so it is visible.

8.3 Dari frekuensi ke termodinamika

8.3 From frequencies to thermodynamics

  • Imajiner = 0: minimum. 1 imajiner: keadaan transisi (periksa bahwa modenya sepanjang koordinat reaksi, lalu IRC). ≥2 imajiner: bukan struktur kimia stabil; optimasi ulang.
  • Imaginary = 0: minimum. 1 imaginary: transition state (check that the mode follows the reaction coordinate, then IRC). ≥2 imaginary: not a stable chemical structure; re-optimise.
  • $G=E_{elec}+ZPE+(H_{corr}\text{ termal})-TS$ menghasilkan energi bebas reaksi dan barrier.
  • $G=E_{elec}+ZPE+(H_{corr}\text{ thermal})-TS$ gives reaction free energies and barriers.
  • Frekuensi hasil DFT biasanya dikalikan faktor skala (≈0,96–0,98 untuk B3LYP, bergantung basis); selalu laporkan faktor dan sumbernya.
  • DFT frequencies are usually multiplied by a scale factor (≈0.96–0.98 for B3LYP, depending on the basis); always report the factor and its source.
  • Frekuensi hanya valid pada geometri hasil optimasi di level teori yang sama.
  • Frequencies are only valid at a geometry optimised at the same level of theory.
